# Governance

## Stewardship

The Trust Requirements Profile specification is stewarded by the AI Trust Alliance, which is being established as an independent standards body for the trust requirements of the AI economy. The Ohio State University is a founding participant. The Alliance is currently organized under Striv AI while its independent legal structure is formed; separation into an independently governed entity, and the governance model that goes with it, is a core deliverable of the current scoping work.

## Decision-Making

Decisions about the TRP specification follow a transparent process:

- **Profiles**: Anyone can author and contribute a domain profile. Profiles carry their own author attribution, version, and license. Contributing a profile does not require approval; it requires validation against the schema.
- **Spec changes**: Changes to the core specification or schema are proposed as GitHub issues and discussed openly. Until the Alliance's formal review process is established, review and approval before merging is handled by the current maintainers.
- **Extension promotion**: A proven extension may be promoted into the core specification through the defined promotion path described in the spec. Promotion decisions will be made by the Alliance's governance process once it is established; until then, they are made by the current maintainers based on demonstrated adoption and technical merit.

## Contribution Workflow

1. Open an issue describing the proposed change or new profile.
2. Discuss with the community.
3. Submit a pull request.
4. For profiles: validation against the schema is sufficient for merge.
5. For spec or schema changes: maintainer review required, pending the Alliance's formal review process.

## Anti-Capture Safeguards

The Alliance is being designed so that no single company, including the original author, will control the standard, and current maintainer decisions are made with that goal in mind. The scoping work funded under the current planning phase will formalize membership tiers, decision rights, multi-stakeholder input requirements, amendment processes, and long-term sustainability mechanisms, drawing on proven models such as the Cloud Security Alliance, the International Association of Privacy Professionals, and the Project Management Institute.

## Current Status

Governance is in its formative stage. This document will be updated as the Alliance formalizes its structure through the scoping process. Feedback on governance design is welcome as a GitHub issue.
