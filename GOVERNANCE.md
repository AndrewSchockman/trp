# Governance

## Stewardship

The Trust Requirements Profile specification is governed by the AI Trust Alliance, an independent standards body established to develop and steward the trust requirements of the AI economy. The Ohio State University is a founding member. The Alliance is separate from any single vendor, so the standard belongs to the ecosystem rather than to its authors.

## Decision-Making

Decisions about the TRP specification follow a transparent process:

- **Profiles**: Anyone can author and contribute a domain profile. Profiles carry their own author attribution, version, and license. Contributing a profile does not require approval; it requires validation against the schema.
- **Spec changes**: Changes to the core specification or schema are proposed as GitHub issues, discussed openly, and require review and approval by the Alliance governance process before merging.
- **Extension promotion**: A proven extension may be promoted into the core specification through the defined promotion path described in the spec. Promotion decisions are made by the Alliance based on demonstrated adoption and technical merit.

## Contribution Workflow

1. Open an issue describing the proposed change or new profile.
2. Discuss with the community.
3. Submit a pull request.
4. For profiles: validation against the schema is sufficient for merge.
5. For spec or schema changes: Alliance review required.

## Anti-Capture Safeguards

The Alliance is designed so that no single company, including the original author, controls the standard. Governance decisions require multi-stakeholder input. The scoping work funded under the current planning phase will formalize membership tiers, decision rights, amendment processes, and long-term sustainability mechanisms, drawing on proven models such as the Cloud Security Alliance, the International Association of Privacy Professionals, and the Project Management Institute.

## Current Status

Governance is in its formative stage. This document will be updated as the Alliance formalizes its structure through the scoping process. Feedback on governance design is welcome as a GitHub issue.
